  5. SAC'25
    Predicting Battery Levels of Sensor Nodes Using Reinforcement Learning in Harsh Underground Mining Environments
    Manish Anand Yadav, Mohamed Elmahallawy, Sanjay Madria, and Samuel Frimpong
    40th ACM/SIGAPP Symposium On Applied Computing (SAC), 2025
    Abs PDF

    We propose PPO-LSTM, a deep reinforcement learning approach for underground mining environments that predicts sensor battery levels using miners' movement and communication dynamics. PPO-LSTM outperforms DDPG and SAC, achieving 4% improvement in MAPE, 1.07% in MAE, and 5-10% in RMSE.

  6. PerCom'24
    Stitching Satellites to the Edge: Pervasive and Efficient Federated LEO Satellite Learning
    Mohamed Elmahallawy, and Tie Luo
    2024 IEEE International Conference on Pervasive Computing and Communications (PerCom), 2024
    Abs HTML PDF

    FL-SEC empowers LEO satellites to execute large-scale ML tasks onboard. Reduces FL convergence time by nearly 30x, satellite energy consumption to 1.38W, with 96% accuracy. Awarded Best Paper Runner-up at IEEE PerCom 2024.

  7. JSAC'24
    Communication-Efficient Federated Learning for LEO Constellations Integrated With HAPs Using Hybrid NOMA-OFDM
    Mohamed Elmahallawy, Tie Luo, and Khaled Ramadan
    IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, Vol. 42(5), 2024
    Abs HTML PDF

    NomaFedHAP utilizes HAPs as distributed parameter servers and introduces NOMA into LEO for fast, bandwidth-efficient model transmissions. Derives closed-form outage probability expressions with superior FL convergence.
